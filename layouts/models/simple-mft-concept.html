Construction stages

  • Box — 805 × 555 × 18 mm
  • Dog holes Sketch — one Circle seed and one native 9 × 7 rectangular pattern create 63 profiles
  • One selective cut uses 59 profiles and leaves four diagonal ring-2 positions open
  • Clamp slots Sketch — one master Slot, one native sketch pattern and three mirrors create 8 Slots
  • Two through-all cuts — one for dog holes and one for clamp slots
  • Chamfer — four straight 20 × 20 mm plan-view corner cuts

Design notes

Background

Model-owned context uses the same heading hierarchy and body typography as every other section.

Reference basis

The Simple MFT worktop is a photo-based study of the CRAFTMAKER ProStation S30 replacement worktop. The model covers the worktop plate, not the complete workbench.

Known dimensions

Published replacement-top dimensions used by the model are:

  • 805 × 555 × 18 mm
  • Ø20 mm dog holes

The front reference supports a 9 × 7 hole grid. The current nominal pitch is 76 mm in X and Y.

Photo-based slot estimate

Eight slots are visible: two in each quadrant. Current nominal values are 24 mm slot width, 80 mm end-cap centre distance, about 104 mm overall length and approximately 45° slot angle.

Reuse

The standalone worktop is also a reusable SCPF component. The assembly variant uses the same worktop definition and adds the generic GuideRail component.

Reference

Reference and 2D expectation

The 76 mm pitch, 20 mm corner cuts and ≈104 × 24 mm slot dimensions are photo-based estimates, not manufacturer specifications.

Front product reference

Reference used to estimate the hole and slot pattern.

2D expectation

Nominal 9 × 7 grid at 76 mm pitch with four diagonal ring-2 holes omitted and two slots per quadrant.
